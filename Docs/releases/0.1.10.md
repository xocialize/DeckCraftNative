DeckCraftNative 0.1.10 draws the effect presets you choose from, and makes exported PDFs cleaner around pictures with transparency. It is still an early preview.

**Requires** macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. Open the DMG and drag DeckCraftNative to Applications, replacing the older version. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. If you run 0.1.0 to 0.1.9, Help › Check for Updates… offers this release.

## New
- **Drawn effect presets.** Format ▸ Effects shows each Shadow, Glow and Soft Edges preset as a small card with the effect drawn on it, instead of a list of names. The preset in use is marked; click another to apply it.

## Fixed
- **PDF export:** a picture with transparent areas, or a shape with a blur or soft edges, no longer shows a thin dark line along its edges in viewers that smooth images.

## Known limitations
- **`.ppt` files** are converted, not edited in place: DeckCraftNative doesn't write `.ppt`. Picture bullets and VBA macros don't come over. PowerPoint 95 and older files aren't supported.
- **Not supported:** files protected with rights management (IRM) or a third-party encryption module.
- **Video:** AV1 needs an M3 or later.
- **Language:** English only.
- **Spotlight:** its search panel finds `.deckcraft` files by name, not by the words inside them; ask Siri for those. `.pptx` and `.ppt` files are indexed by macOS itself.
- **Quick Look:** for `.pptx` and `.ppt` files it shows macOS's own Office preview.

The full list is in [EVALUATION.md](https://github.com/xocialize/DeckCraftNative/blob/main/EVALUATION.md).
