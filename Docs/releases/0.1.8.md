DeckCraftNative 0.1.8 opens PowerPoint 97–2003 presentations (`.ppt`). It is still an early preview.

**Requires** macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. Open the DMG and drag DeckCraftNative to Applications, replacing the older version. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. If you run 0.1.0 to 0.1.7, Help › Check for Updates… offers this release.

## New
- **Opening `.ppt` files.** A presentation saved by PowerPoint 97 to 2003, or by a later PowerPoint in that format (`.ppt`, `.pot`, `.pps`), opens as a converted copy, as PowerPoint does with an old file. Save it as a `.pptx` (or `.deckcraft`); the original isn't changed.
- **What comes over:** the slides and their notes; text with its formatting, bullets and the template's text styles; shapes, groups, freeforms, connectors, tables and WordArt, with their fills, lines and shadows; pictures (old Mac PICT pictures are converted), picture and pattern fills, and backgrounds; the template's own graphics (logos, bars and lines on the slide master and title master), which a slide hides when it says so; headers, footers and hyperlinks; comments; transitions, hidden slides and animations, which play in the slide show.
- **What becomes a picture:** embedded objects such as charts and equations come over as the picture PowerPoint saved with them.
- **Password-protected `.ppt` files** ask for their password, as PowerPoint does; a file protected with PowerPoint's default password opens without asking. A typed password is kept, so Save As `.pptx` protects the new file with it.
- **What the status line says:** when a `.ppt` opens, the status line says what was converted and anything that wasn't, such as a slide that couldn't be read.

## For scripts
- `file.open` on a `.ppt` replies with `converted`: the slide count, any slides that couldn't be read, the objects that became pictures and what isn't brought over.
- `dccli` reads `.ppt` files everywhere it reads `.pptx`: `info`, `render`, `convert`.

## Known limitations
- **`.ppt` files** are converted, not edited in place: DeckCraftNative doesn't write `.ppt`. Picture bullets and VBA macros don't come over. PowerPoint 95 and older files aren't supported.
- **Not supported:** files protected with rights management (IRM) or a third-party encryption module.
- **Video:** AV1 needs an M3 or later.
- **Language:** English only.
- **Spotlight:** its search panel finds `.deckcraft` files by name, not by the words inside them; ask Siri for those. `.pptx` and `.ppt` files are indexed by macOS itself.
- **Quick Look:** for `.pptx` and `.ppt` files it shows macOS's own Office preview.
