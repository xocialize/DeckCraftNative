DeckCraftNative 0.1.11 works better with VoiceOver and other assistive tools, after an audit with Apple's Accessibility Inspector. It is still an early preview.

**Requires** macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. Open the DMG and drag DeckCraftNative to Applications, replacing the older version. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. If you run 0.1.0 to 0.1.10, Help › Check for Updates… offers this release.

## Fixed
- **VoiceOver names the parts of the window.** The ribbon, the inspector, the status bar, the start window and dialogs are announced by name as you move into them.
- **Slide Sorter with VoiceOver:** a slide can be selected, and a section folded or unfolded, with VoiceOver's press (Control-Option-Space).
- **Format Painter in the inspector** is a button and a separate "Format Painter options" menu, so each half has a name.
- **Easier to read:** section slide counts in the slide navigator and Slide Sorter, and slide numbers in the Outline, are drawn with stronger contrast.

## Known limitations
- **`.ppt` files** are converted, not edited in place: DeckCraftNative doesn't write `.ppt`. Picture bullets and VBA macros don't come over. PowerPoint 95 and older files aren't supported.
- **Not supported:** files protected with rights management (IRM) or a third-party encryption module.
- **Video:** AV1 needs an M3 or later.
- **Language:** English only.
- **Spotlight:** its search panel finds `.deckcraft` files by name, not by the words inside them; ask Siri for those. `.pptx` and `.ppt` files are indexed by macOS itself.
- **Quick Look:** for `.pptx` and `.ppt` files it shows macOS's own Office preview.

The full list is in [EVALUATION.md](https://github.com/xocialize/DeckCraftNative/blob/main/EVALUATION.md).
