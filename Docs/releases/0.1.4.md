DeckCraftNative 0.1.4 adds a ribbon and a start window, so the app feels familiar to anyone who knows PowerPoint. It is still an early preview.

**Requires** macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. Open the DMG and drag DeckCraftNative to Applications, replacing the older version. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. If you run 0.1.0 to 0.1.3, Help › Check for Updates… offers this release.

## What's new
- **The ribbon.** Tabs of commands above the slide: Home, Insert, Draw, Design, Transitions, Animations, Slide Show, Record, Review and View.
  - **Contextual tabs** appear when the selection calls for them: Shape Format, Picture Format, Table Design and Layout, SmartArt Design, Chart Design, Playback, and the Slide, Notes and Handout Master tabs. They are tinted with the accent colour.
  - **Galleries** of themes, variants, transitions, animations and styles show as tiles. The "›" button opens the whole gallery.
  - **The Quick Access Toolbar** sits in the window's toolbar, beside Search, Present, Comments and a new Share button.
  - **Narrow windows:** groups shrink and then collapse into a single button as the window narrows. Tabs that don't fit move under "»".
  - **Collapse the Ribbon (⌘F1):** clicking a tab then shows its commands over the slide.
  - **Keyboard:** with Full Keyboard Access on, F6 moves to the ribbon. The arrow keys move between commands, and Esc returns to the slide.
  - **Tooltips:** every command has a short description, which also shows in the command palette.
- **Which layout you get.** New installs start with the ribbon. If you have used an earlier version, you keep the toolbar and inspector you know. Switch at any time with View › Ribbon (⌥⌘R) or in Settings › General › Window layout. The app remembers your layout, the selected tab, whether the ribbon is collapsed, and the Quick Access Toolbar.
- **The start window.** Opening the app with nothing to open shows a start window. It has Home, New and Open pages, theme and template tiles, a search field and your recent files. You can turn it off in Settings › General.
- **File › Open Recent** lists your recent presentations.
- **Fixed:** with always-visible scroll bars, zooming in past the window could shift the slide off its middle.

## Known limitations
- **Video:** AV1 needs an M3 or later.
- **Language:** English only.
- **Spotlight:** it doesn't index the text of `.deckcraft` files yet.
- **Quick Look:** for `.pptx` files it shows macOS's own Office preview.
- **Not supported:** legacy `.ppt` files and password-protected `.pptx` files.

The full list is in [EVALUATION.md](https://github.com/xocialize/DeckCraftNative/blob/main/EVALUATION.md).
