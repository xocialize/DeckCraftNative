DeckCraftNative 0.1.12 speaks French, Italian, German and Spanish, as preview translations. It is still an early preview.

**Requires** macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. Open the DMG and drag DeckCraftNative to Applications, replacing the older version. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. If you run 0.1.0 to 0.1.11, Help › Check for Updates… offers this release.

## New
- **French, Italian, German and Spanish** (preview). The menus, ribbon, inspector, dialogs, tooltips, Siri and Shortcuts phrases and Quick Look are translated, using PowerPoint's own terms in each language. DeckCraftNative follows your Mac's preferred language. To choose a language for this app alone, use System Settings › General › Language & Region › Applications.
- **These translations haven't been reviewed by native speakers yet.** If something reads oddly, or you'd like to help review a language, see [issue #1](https://github.com/xocialize/DeckCraftNative/issues/1). Reviewing needs only a spreadsheet.

## Known limitations
- **`.ppt` files** are converted, not edited in place: DeckCraftNative doesn't write `.ppt`. Picture bullets and VBA macros don't come over. PowerPoint 95 and older files aren't supported.
- **Not supported:** files protected with rights management (IRM) or a third-party encryption module.
- **Video:** AV1 needs an M3 or later.
- **Languages:** French, Italian, German and Spanish are previews that native speakers haven't reviewed yet. Text from the presentation engine, such as "Click to add title" on a new slide and error messages, stays in English.
- **Spotlight:** its search panel finds `.deckcraft` files by name, not by the words inside them; ask Siri for those. `.pptx` and `.ppt` files are indexed by macOS itself.
- **Quick Look:** for `.pptx` and `.ppt` files it shows macOS's own Office preview.

The full list is in [EVALUATION.md](https://github.com/xocialize/DeckCraftNative/blob/main/EVALUATION.md).
