DeckCraftNative 0.1.6 lets Spotlight and Siri find your presentations, and fixes the Outline view. It is still an early preview.

**Requires** macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. Open the DMG and drag DeckCraftNative to Applications, replacing the older version. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. If you run 0.1.0 to 0.1.5, Help › Check for Updates… offers this release.

## New
- **Spotlight and Siri find your presentations.** Each `.deckcraft` file you open or save in DeckCraftNative is added to Spotlight:
  - Spotlight finds it by its name, and choosing it opens it in DeckCraftNative.
  - Siri finds it by what its slides, notes and comments say, and opens it, for example "open my presentation that mentions the quarterly survey".
  - Files you haven't opened in 0.1.6 or later aren't added. A file that has been deleted is removed the next time the app starts, and one you don't open for a year drops out.
  - Tested on macOS 27.

## Fixed
- **Rows cut off in the Outline view.** With always-visible scroll bars, the Outline's rows could keep heights measured before the scroll bar appeared, and clip their text.
- **For scripts:** the control channel's `ui.screenshot` returned an old picture of a DeckCraftNative window while another app's window covered it. It now draws a covered window itself, and its reply says how the picture was taken (`source`).

## Known limitations
- **Video:** AV1 needs an M3 or later.
- **Language:** English only.
- **Spotlight:** its search panel finds `.deckcraft` files by name, not by the words inside them; ask Siri for those. `.pptx` files are indexed by macOS itself.
- **Quick Look:** for `.pptx` files it shows macOS's own Office preview.
- **Not supported:** legacy `.ppt` files and password-protected `.pptx` files.

The full list is in [EVALUATION.md](https://github.com/xocialize/DeckCraftNative/blob/main/EVALUATION.md).
