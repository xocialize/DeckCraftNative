DeckCraftNative 0.1.9 brings DeckCraft's latest changes, opens some slow `.ppt` files much faster, and fixes long titles in the Outline. It is still an early preview.

**Requires** macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. Open the DMG and drag DeckCraftNative to Applications, replacing the older version. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. If you run 0.1.0 to 0.1.8, Help › Check for Updates… offers this release.

## New
- **Drag several slides at once** in the slide navigator and the Slide Sorter: select them, then drag any one of them.
- **Slide background fill** for shapes and text boxes (Format ▸ Fill), which shows the slide's background through the shape.
- **Shadow Size** (1–200 %) in Format ▸ Effects ▸ Shadow.
- **Scale Height and Width, and Position ▸ From** (top-left corner or centre) in Format ▸ Size, for a single shape.
- **Effect presets:** the Shadow picker shows the preset a shadow came from; Glow and Soft Edges have preset sizes, and a glow preset keeps the glow's colour. A gradient's flip is kept when you edit the fill.
- **Slide show:** going back plays the slide's transition in reverse; a click on a shape that an animation has moved or scaled lands where the shape is shown; **Keep mouse cursor visible** is a Set Up Slide Show option; presenter view and its Next slide preview no longer slow the show down.

## Fixed
- **Some `.ppt` files** with complex old pictures or many fonts took 40 to 90 seconds to open; they now take a few seconds.
- **Outline:** a long slide title is wrapped onto as many lines as it needs instead of being cut off at the column's edge.
- **Text:** symbol-font bullets show a bullet, never a missing-glyph box; "Start at" restarts a numbered list; long dates follow the language's format; variable fonts are found under the names Windows gives them.
- **`.pptx`:** line miter limits and alignment, gradient flips and bullet settings that follow the text are kept; Auto-reverse, media settings, picture placeholders and a few chart settings come over from PowerPoint's files as PowerPoint shows them.
- **Charts:** a histogram value on a bin edge is counted in that bin.
- **Editing:** duplicating and pasting name the copies as PowerPoint does, groups follow their contents, formatting on layouts and masters reaches their slides, and spelling checks all-caps words.

## For scripts
- New command **`slide.actions`** lists a slide's click and hover actions and its text hyperlinks without running them (331 commands).
- Commands that take a slide or shape index now refuse an index that doesn't exist instead of using the selection.
- MCP: `insert_chart` takes a box, and very large messages no longer end the session. `dccli run --cmd-file` reads commands from a file.

## Known limitations
- **`.ppt` files** are converted, not edited in place: DeckCraftNative doesn't write `.ppt`. Picture bullets and VBA macros don't come over. PowerPoint 95 and older files aren't supported.
- **Not supported:** files protected with rights management (IRM) or a third-party encryption module.
- **Video:** AV1 needs an M3 or later.
- **Language:** English only.
- **Spotlight:** its search panel finds `.deckcraft` files by name, not by the words inside them; ask Siri for those. `.pptx` and `.ppt` files are indexed by macOS itself.
- **Quick Look:** for `.pptx` and `.ppt` files it shows macOS's own Office preview.
