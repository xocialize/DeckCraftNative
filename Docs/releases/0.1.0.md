DeckCraftNative 0.1.0 is the first build of a native macOS presentation editor, ported from [DeckCraft](https://github.com/storytold/deckcraft) by the ArtCraft team. It's an early preview, not a finished product.

**Requires** macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. Open the DMG and drag DeckCraftNative to Applications. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

## What it does
- **Build decks** with themes, layouts, text, shapes, pictures, tables, charts, SmartArt, transitions and animations. It saves `.deckcraft` files, which open in DeckCraft too.
- **Open and save PowerPoint files** (`.pptx`; it also opens `.potx` and `.ppsx`). The files it writes open in Microsoft PowerPoint without repair.
- **Present** full screen, with transitions drawn on the GPU, builds, media, and a presenter view on your second display.
- **Share** PDF of slides, notes pages or handouts with selectable text, PNG and JPEG images, and animated GIF. **Print** with the same layouts.
- **Review** with comments, replies and resolved threads, spelling, and the thesaurus.
- **Mac integration:** Quick Look previews and thumbnails of `.deckcraft` files, Shortcuts actions (export, outline, slide count, new presentation, add slide, start the show), and Check for Updates.
- **Automation:** every menu command can be driven from scripts through the `--control PORT` channel or MCP. The `dccli` command-line tool is in the repository.

## Known limitations
- **Language:** English only. The interface is ready for translation.
- **Video:** VP8 and VP9 videos play their sound but not their picture. AV1 needs an M3 or later.
- **Spotlight:** it doesn't index the text of `.deckcraft` files yet, because macOS doesn't call the app's importer.
- **Quick Look:** for `.pptx` files it shows macOS's own Office preview rather than DeckCraftNative's.
- **Not supported:** legacy `.ppt` files and password-protected `.pptx` files.

The full list is in [EVALUATION.md](https://github.com/xocialize/DeckCraftNative/blob/main/EVALUATION.md).
