DeckCraftNative 0.1.15 lets you change a chart's title, legend, data labels and gridlines. It is still an early preview.

**Requires** macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. Open the DMG and drag DeckCraftNative to Applications, replacing the older version. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. If you run 0.1.0 to 0.1.14, Help › Check for Updates… offers this release.

## New
- **Chart Design › Add Chart Element**, as in PowerPoint:
  - Chart Title: None or Above Chart.
  - Legend: None, Right, Top, Left or Bottom.
  - Data Labels: None or Show.
  - Gridlines: None or Major Gridlines.
- **Format › Chart › Chart Elements** in the inspector:
  - a Chart Title switch and the title's text, which you can edit there;
  - the legend's position;
  - switches for data labels and gridlines.

  Each change is one undo step. Before, a chart's title could only be set in PowerPoint.

## Known limitations
- **Chart titles** can't be edited on the slide yet; use the inspector.
- **`.ppt` files** are converted, not edited in place: DeckCraftNative doesn't write `.ppt`. Picture bullets and VBA macros don't come over. PowerPoint 95 and older files aren't supported.
- **Not supported:** files protected with rights management (IRM) or a third-party encryption module.
- **Video:** AV1 needs an M3 or later.
- **Languages:** French, Italian, German and Spanish are previews that native speakers haven't reviewed yet. Text from the presentation engine, such as "Click to add title" on a new slide and error messages, stays in English.
- **Spotlight:** its search panel finds `.deckcraft` files by name, not by the words inside them; ask Siri for those. `.pptx` and `.ppt` files are indexed by macOS itself.
- **Quick Look:** for `.pptx` and `.ppt` files it shows macOS's own Office preview.

The full list is in [EVALUATION.md](https://github.com/xocialize/DeckCraftNative/blob/main/EVALUATION.md).
